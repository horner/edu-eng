# BIO-042 — Studio and assessment evidence

Date: 2026-10-25

Authored eight [studios](../../data/studios/) and 16 [assessments](../../data/assessments/), linked through each term and competency. Studios progress from an assistive fixture to a calibrated measurement bench, instrumentation, a biomechanical component, a physiological analogue, an integrated educational device and a two-term capstone.

Every studio defines a need, deliverables, owned decomposition, interfaces, rotating responsibilities, early experiments, a scenario-specific failure investigation, an individual defense and a retrospective with a process change and follow-up. Short regular explanations cover accomplished work, current work, learning and blockers. Sprint ceremonies are adapted to physical experiments. Huddle and Pulse are optional configured link-outs; they do not gate browsing or assessment. A Pulse update can include Beats, but counts or video length are not competence evidence.

The individual assessment combines a supervised quantitative task, reproducible analysis, instructor follow-up questions, contribution/interface evidence and an unfamiliar transfer task. Individual rubrics score technical reasoning, reproducibility, explanation/transfer and accountable collaboration on 0–3 dimensions. Team rubrics separately score integration, decomposition/interfaces, inclusive leadership/communication and learning from failure. Proposed gates require at least 2 in each dimension plus correction/reassessment; thresholds require faculty calibration. Team success cannot mark every member individually competent.

Brilliant, relevant certifications or problem-bank results may support preparation and external assessment. They do not automatically replace the specified individual performance evidence. Five Whys is used to generate hypotheses; competing causes require discriminating tests, and corrective actions require verification. A retrospective's chosen change is followed into the next iteration.
