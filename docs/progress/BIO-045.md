# BIO-045 — Make room for the joy and the why

Date: 2026-10-26

The user expected A Capella Science’s molecular-shape and Evo-Devo songs, plus Amoeba Sisters, as inviting ways to understand why a subject matters. None was linked in the initial 49-resource catalog. The earlier readings were useful references, but the curation had underdelivered on this part of the requested experience.

## Added and connected

Two separate curation agents supplied six new records: five creator-original videos and an OpenStax molecular-geometry follow-up. The complete English transcripts of all five new videos were exported/read, selected scientific mechanisms checked against the recorded references, and sampled frames inspected for both songs. Each record separates observed engagement from accuracy and states its limits. Full audiovisual viewing, learner enjoyment, classroom playback and faculty approval are not claimed.

- **Chemistry:** The Molecular Shape of You; Amoeba Sisters’ Properties of Water and Biomolecules. The song leads to physical models and the visible molecular-geometry reading.
- **Biology:** optional Evo-Devo context, the Amoeba Sisters cell tour and biomolecules, with stories, predictions and drawing/model games.
- Existing calculus/Fourier/physiology videos now also have explicit curiosity roles and reasons to care. The Fourier video's metadata-only label remains visible.

The current catalog has 55 records (47 selected-text reviews, seven transcript reviews, one metadata-only video). Eight `learning_role: spark` entries directly cover four of the 24 local units; the remaining 20 still need this curation pass. The [resource audit](../resource-audit.md) and [backlog](../follow-up-backlog.md) make that gap explicit.

## Learner experience and contributor contract

**Start with curiosity** appears before prerequisite/reference material in the chemistry/biology detail. **Why this matters**, the creator-original link and **Play with the idea** remain visible; source/assessment/access details are available in **Source review and teaching notes**. The global resource library also puts sparks first, searches creator/provider names and retains deeper resources afterward. Explicit chapter resource order sets the starting hook. The site remains static with external link-outs.

The schema validates optional learning roles and requires `why_it_matters` for a spark. A negative fixture rejects a missing reason or an unsupported role while preserving compatibility with existing records. The [curation skill](../../.agents/skills/curate-learning-resources/SKILL.md) now seeks motivating entry points and records missing hooks rather than treating a set of readings as the whole experience.
