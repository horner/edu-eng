# BIO-101 — Evidence-based follow-up scope

Date: 2026-10-25

The [follow-up backlog](../follow-up-backlog.md) turns demonstrated gaps into priorities with a proposed role owner and observable acceptance condition. It covers academic equivalence/credits, living-system acquisition, rubric calibration, a measured pilot, catalog prerequisite ambiguity, resource depth and video review, staffing/facilities, front-end packaging, optional interchange and progress portability.

The backlog is grounded in the [ABET audit](../abet-audit.md), [resource audit](../resource-audit.md), [catalog source note](../sources/indiana-tech-bme-2026-2027.md), [independent review](../independent-review.md), and the actual build/browser observations. Proposed owners are roles rather than unapproved assignments to people. Faculty feedback is explicitly pending and will revise the scope after the prototype is shared.

The current PoC acceptance concerns a working, inspectable proposal. It does not mark academic approval, actual attainment, institutional support or native roadmap.sh compatibility complete. Those limits remain visible in source data, interface and review documents.
